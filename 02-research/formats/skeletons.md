# The 4 skeletons (almost every winner fits one)

1. **Pain funnel** — slide 1 hits a specific pain; 2–3 "this is literally me"; 4 app enters softly; 5–6 show it working.
2. **List** — 5–7 tips, one per slide. Gets saved. Never end on "thanks for reading" — end on the app.
3. **Identity** — who the viewer wants to become. Aesthetic images, short lines. Lower peaks, very consistent.
4. **Quick hit** — 2–3 slides, bottom of funnel.

Promote a structure to its own format file once it shows up on 3+ different accounts with 50k+ views and 3x the account median, posted in the last 30 days.
