# Fit board / finds grid (SMOKE)

Reference: @closetinspo ("Calm fits", 15.3k likes / 4,331 saves), @cjfits (13.5k likes / 4,755 saves).
- Real product photos, backgrounds removed, laid out flat on a plain background (grey #5F646B or white).
- One full outfit per slide: top, bottom, shoes + 1–4 extras (watch, bracelet, beanie, fragrance, phone, car).
- No text on slides (or one tiny label). Caption carries the vibe ("Calm fits⚜️").
- 6 slides. Rotate category, not just colour.
- Real brands only. Never let ChatGPT regenerate products (it changes logos) — use real photos.
- Last slide or caption → app/waitlist.
