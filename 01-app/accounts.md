## Anti-"posting machine" rules
- Shift each time by a random 5–15 min every day. Never exact on the hour.
- 2nd post of the day: 10-min warm-up (scroll, like, reply), not 25.
- Never post the same set, caption or hashtags on two accounts.
- Never delete and repost. A flop stays up.
- Write a fresh caption every post; no copy-paste blocks.
- Switch accounts only after finishing (post + reply to first comments).
- Stay inside the app for a minute after posting; don't post and close.

## Weekend
- Sat: same slots; evening is strongest.
- Sun: softest day; keep slots, don't panic on lower views.

## Avoid
- 14:00–17:00 weekdays (lowest engagement).
- Monday before 09:00.

## Rule
After 2 weeks: each account → Analytics → Followers → follower activity. Switch to its own peak hours. Log changes here.
