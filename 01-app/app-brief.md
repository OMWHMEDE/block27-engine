# App brief — BLOCK27

## One sentence
An AI stylist for the clothes you already own: you show it your closet, it builds the outfits.

## Who it's for
Men who own decent clothes but wear the same few things, don't know what goes with what, and want to look put-together without buying more. (Refine in pain-bank interview.)

## The magic moment ("wait, I need that")
Seeing an outfit built entirely from clothes they already own — pieces they forgot they had — and realising they didn't need to buy anything.
On a phone screen: closet photos → one tap → a full outfit with a short, cold explanation of why it works.

## Brand line
"You own good clothes. You wear them wrong."

## Status
Pre-launch iOS app (Expo). Apple enrolment Nov 6, 2026. Waitlist at block27.app. Real screenshots come from Expo Go → `01-app/assets/`.

## Top pains (draft — confirm in interview)
1. Full closet, nothing to wear

3. Doesn't know what goes with what
4. Buys clothes that never get worn
5. Wastes time every morning deciding
6. Wants to look expensive on a normal budget
7. Has nice pieces but outfits feel "off"
8. Doesn't know which colours suit / match
9. Copies fits online, never looks the same
10. Dressing for an event and panicking

## Objections (draft)

2. "AI can't know my style"
3. "I'd have to photograph everything"
4. "Privacy — photos of my stuff"
5. "Another subscription"
