# IG Cartoon Engine

Faceless cartoon story videos for Instagram Reels (and YouTube Shorts). **Never TikTok.**
Read this file + results/winners.md + results/failure-log.md before writing any episode.

## Accounts & times (GMT)
- IG 1 — standalone true clothing stories. 2 posts/day: 13:00 and 20:00
- IG 2 — standalone true clothing stories. 1 post/day: 18:00
- IG 3 — one ongoing SERIES (jeans / Levi's 1873 story, multi-episode). 1 post/day: 22:00
Each account has its own stories — never post the same video on two accounts.

## The character & style (paste as ChatGPT setup / Project instructions)
Same yellow character every image: graphic-novel cel style, bold ink outlines, flat colour, amber rim light, cinematic. Vertical 9:16. No text in images. Keep him identical in every image. Outfit and setting listed per episode.

## Story rules
- **True stories only, facts verified** (dates, names, numbers, quotes) with sources in the episode file.
- Stories about **clothes** — a piece, a rule, a scandal, an invention.
- There must be **stakes**: something happens TO the character (danger, chase, scandal, a bet). A list of facts = a history lesson = flop.
- Framings: "the man who…" (third person, character plays the real person) or present-tense "you" POV. The "you wake up in…" opener is worn out — don't default to it.
- Hook in the first second: sound + image + line together. **Never** open with "stop" or "imagine". Present-tense jolt or a date.
- Length 25–45s (IG allows up to ~45s).

## Ending (LOCKED) — bridge, then CTA. Never jump straight to the CTA.
1. Bridge line tying the story to clothes meaning something, e.g. "Clothes were never just clothes. They tell people who you are before you say a word."
2. Then exactly:
"So what are yours saying?
You don't have to know. BLOCK27 will say it louder for you.
And you're probably wondering what BLOCK27 is. It's an AI stylist for the clothes you already own. You show it your closet, and it builds the outfits.
It's not out yet. Join the waitlist at block27.app, and you'll know the second it drops."

## Edit rules
- No black frames at the start. Hook in the first second.
- Captions burned in. Slow zoom on every frame. New shot every 2–3s. Slightly brighter.
- CTA only at the end. Don't end on app screenshots. Last frame loops to the first.
- Free assets: Pixabay SFX/music, Mixkit, DaVinci Resolve, Upscayl (4x) before editing at 2160p.
- Turn on Instagram's AI label (self-disclose). No photoreal faces.

## Every episode file contains (ig-cartoons/episodes/YYYY-MM-DD-account-slug.md)
1. Account + slot
2. Full voiceover (always resend the FULL version after any change)
3. ChatGPT setup message (character outfit + setting for this episode)
4. Numbered image prompts (12–15), one per 2–3s
5. Which VO line goes with which image
6. Sound cues
7. Caption
8. Sources
