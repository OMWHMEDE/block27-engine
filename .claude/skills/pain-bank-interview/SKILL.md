description: Interview HMEDE one question at a time to finish 01-app/app-brief.md, then build the pain bank and personas.

Ask ONE short question at a time. Cover: who it's for, the magic moment on a phone screen, top 10 pains, top 5 objections, competitors, life after.
Then collect 50 real phrases people use for the problem (from our post comments, Reddit/TikTok comments he shares, his own words), quoted exactly, grouped by theme → Pain bank section of app-brief.md.
Update personas.md (one per account cluster).
